1. Effect of WO₃ loading on the surface acidity of WO₃/Al₂O₃ composite oxides

作者：Z.-Q. Hu, Z.-Q. Huang, R. Zhang, J. Jagiello, J. F. ... , A. Datye, J. A. Schwarz

刊物：Applied Catalysis A: General, 84 (1992) 123–139

机构：Syracuse University (美国雪城大学)

Abstract: High-resolution transmission electron microscopy, X-ray energy dispersive spectroscopy, colorimetric titration and inverse gas chromatography (IGC) were used to study the changes in acidity of a series of WO₃/Al₂O₃ composite oxides with increasing loading of tungsten. The addition of WO₃ increases the Lewis and Brønsted acidity of the series of composites.

2. Surface Area Determination of Supported Oxides: WO₃/Al₂O₃

作者：R. L. Brady, D. Southmayd, C. Contescu, B. Zhang, J. A. Schwarz

刊物：Journal of Catalysis, 129 (1991) 195–201

机构：Syracuse University (美国雪城大学)

Abstract: The concept that the variation in the point of zero charge (pzc) of physical mixtures of pure oxides can serve as a “calibration curve” to evaluate the surface areas of the individual phases of composite oxides is applied to the WO₃/Al₂O₃ system. Results of two models — whose parameters are the number of dispersed clusters and their size — show the same qualitative trends and become quantitatively the same as the second-phase loading decreases.

3. The Effect of Second-Phase Oxides on the Catalytic Properties of Dispersed Metals: Cobalt Supported on 12% WO₃/Al₂O₃

作者：R. Zhang, J. A. Schwarz, A. Datye, J. P. Baltrus

刊物：Journal of Catalysis, 135 (1992) 200–222

机构：Syracuse University; University of New Mexico; U.S. DOE Pittsburgh Energy Technology Center

Abstract: The differences in the amphoteric properties of WO₃ and Al₂O₃ were exploited: tungsten oxide precursors were selectively mounted on the composite to prepare dispersed cobalt catalysts. Temperature-programmed reduction (TPR), transmission electron microscopy (TEM), X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) and H₂/CO methanation as a test reaction were used; the properties of the resulting catalysts were significantly different from those on the pure oxides.

4. The Adsorption/Impregnation of Pd(II) Cations on Alumina, Silica, and Their Composite Oxides

作者：J. A. Schwarz, C. T. Ugbor, R. Zhang

刊物：Journal of Catalysis, 138 (1992) 38–54

机构：Syracuse University (美国雪城大学)

Abstract: Two models are proposed to evaluate the electric charge structure of alumina, silica and alumina–silica composite oxides at pH and ionic strength conditions identical to those employed during Pd(II) cation adsorption/impregnation. From these models the inventory of negative charge on each support is determined; the model treating the composite as a simple additive contribution of each pure phase is consistent with the strongly adsorbed Pd(NH₃)₄²⁺ amounts measured from adsorption isotherms.

5. The Effect of Second-Phase Oxides on the Catalytic Properties of Dispersed Metals: Palladium Supported on 12% WO₃/Al₂O₃

作者：R. Zhang, J. A. Schwarz, A. Datye, J. P. Baltrus

刊物：Journal of Catalysis, 138 (1992) 55–69

机构：Syracuse University; University of New Mexico; U.S. DOE Pittsburgh Energy Technology Center

Abstract: The earlier study of dispersed cobalt on a 12% WO₃/Al₂O₃ composite oxide (J. Catal. 135, 200 (1992)) is extended to palladium. Temperature-programmed reduction (TPR), transmission electron microscopy (TEM), X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) and catalyst testing were used to characterize the supported palladium catalysts.

6. Structure and phase-separation behavior of Yukawa mixtures studied by the mean-spherical approximation and computer calculations

作者：C. Hoheisel, R. Zhang

刊物：Physical Review A, 43, No. 10 (15 May 1991)

机构：Ruhr-Universität Bochum (德国鲁尔大学)

Abstract: Nearly ideal and very nonideal binary Yukawa mixtures are studied with Monte Carlo (MC) and mean-spherical approximation (MSA) methods. Compressibility factors, static structure factors and local mole fractions are compared in detail; MSA predicts these properties in sufficiently good agreement, and the phase instability is also well predicted. The mean-spherical theory is a suitable tool for describing the thermodynamics of these two-component systems reliably.

7. Extended Kinetic Isotope Method — Its Application to the Analysis of Butene Oxidative Dehydrogenation Reaction Network over Three Catalysts

作者：Zhang Ru (张叡), Shi Xiaoyu (施孝通), Qi Lianqin (齐造动), Yin Yuangen (尹元根)

刊物：Scientia Sinica (Series B) 《中国科学》B辑, Vol. XXIX, No. 7 (July 1986)

机构：Lanzhou Institute of Chemical Physics, Academia Sinica (中国科学院兰州化学物理研究所)

Abstract: The extended kinetic isotope method (E-KIN) is presented in detail and applied to the analysis of the butene oxidative dehydrogenation reaction network over three catalysts, resolving the reaction pathways and kinetics of the network.
